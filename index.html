The Problem

When the environment is dangerous, distance matters.

Many industrial facilities require workers to inspect spaces where gas leaks, chemical spills or unknown structural hazards may be present. Sending a human inside without prior visual and sensor intelligence creates unnecessary physical risk.

THE DIFFERENCE ARGUS CREATES

FROM ENTERING THE HAZARD TO SEEING IT FROM A DISTANCE.

ARGUS changes how hazardous environments are inspected — giving operators visibility and information before they have to send a person inside.

01 / Before ARGUS

Send a person in.

Person → Hazardous area → Manual inspection
  • Manual inspection Visual check requires physical entry into unknown areas.
  • Limited visibility No real-time sensor array or camera stream prior to entry.
  • Direct exposure Personnel exposed to potential toxic gas or structural risks.
  • Slower information Data gathered only after someone travels inside the facility.
Without ARGUS → With ARGUS
02 / With ARGUS

See before you enter.

ARGUS Rover → Camera + Sensors → AI Perception → Operator
  • Remote visual inspection Live optical stream sent directly to the operator station.
  • AI-assisted perception Real-time YOLO object detection identifying hazards.
  • Gas / hazard monitoring Environmental sensing performed before human entry.
  • Real-time information Zero-delay telemetry keeps operators informed at a distance.
The Result

Keep people out of the hazard.
Bring the information to them.

Remote visibility. Intelligent perception. Safer inspection.
